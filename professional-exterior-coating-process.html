The RDS exterior coating process

The exact scope depends on the home, surface condition, coating system, and manufacturer requirements. This is the professional structure RDS uses to help homeowners understand what a preparation-first exterior project should include.

  1. Inspection

    RDS reviews stucco, trim, cracks, chalking, moisture-prone areas, color goals, and access needs.

  2. Surface Cleaning

    Exterior surfaces are cleaned so dirt, organic buildup, and loose material are not trapped below the finish.

  3. Crack & Stucco Repair

    Cracks and damaged stucco areas are addressed where the project requires repair before coating.

  4. Preparation & Protection

    Windows, doors, exterior features, landscaping, and work areas are protected for a cleaner jobsite.

  5. Primer/Surface Conditioning Where Required

    Primers, sealers, or surface conditioning are used where appropriate for the product and surface.

  6. Professional Coating Application

    A professional exterior coating system is applied according to project conditions and product direction.

  7. Trim & Detail Finish

    Trim, edges, doors, and finish details are completed with care so the home looks polished.

  8. Final Review & Inspection

    RDS reviews final details and workmanship before project closeout.

Why process matters in Florida

Florida homes deal with heat, storms, intense sun, humidity, irrigation overspray, and stucco movement. A rushed exterior job can miss the conditions that affect durability. A professional process makes the work easier to understand and helps the finish system start from a better-prepared surface.

  • Moisture-prone walls can be reviewed before product selection.
  • Cracks can be handled before they become finish-quality issues.
  • Cleaning helps reduce the risk of coating over contaminants.
  • Final review gives homeowners a clear closeout point.
