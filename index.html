Exterior Protection Process

The RDS Exterior Protection System

A system-based approach for Florida stucco homes, designed around professional preparation, moisture-aware repairs, and a weather-resistant finish.

1

Exterior inspection

Review the exterior condition, stucco surfaces, cracks, trim, moisture-prone areas, and finish goals.

2

Pressure washing / surface cleaning

Clean exterior surfaces so preparation and coating work can start from a sound base.

3

Crack and stucco repair

Address visible cracks and damaged stucco areas before the finish system is applied.

4

Surface preparation and sealing

Prepare and seal exterior surfaces where appropriate to support a professional coating system.

5

Sherwin-Williams professional masonry coating system

Use a professional masonry coating system selected for the surface, project scope, and application needs.

6

Heat-reflective coating option

Discuss heat-reflective exterior coating options where appropriate for the home and project goals.

7

Final inspection and workmanship review

Review the completed finish, details, and workmanship before closing out the project.

RDS backs its workmanship with a limited workmanship warranty where applicable. Product warranties are subject to the manufacturer’s written terms and proper application requirements.
